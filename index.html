A tiny shop. A clever puzzle.

Arrange 24 products on a 5 × 6 shelf, discover which neighbours sell together, and watch each 25-second customer rush test your layout.

Aislecraft is a single-player game for iPhone and iPad. It plays offline and needs no account. Choose Classic runs with three difficulties, the shared Daily Store, or relaxed Practice.
