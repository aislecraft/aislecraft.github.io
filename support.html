Support

Help with Aislecraft for iPhone and iPad.

Contact

Write to okan.geter@gmail.com. Include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version and the screen where the problem happened. Never send passwords, verification codes or payment details.

How do I learn the rules?
Open Settings › How to play in the game. The tutorial on Home walks through buying, placing and your first rush.
How do I restore a purchase?
Open Store › Restore purchases while signed in with the Apple Account you used to buy Premium or the Neon Mart theme.
What does Premium include?
Premium removes all ads and lets you claim the same optional Classic helpers that rewarded videos offer, without watching a video. It does not change scores or the Daily Store.
My progress is missing on a new device.
Turn on iCloud sync in Settings on both devices and sign in with the same Apple Account. Completed progress syncs; a run in progress stays on the device where you started it.
When does the Daily Store change?
Every day at 00:00 UTC. Everyone plays the same shop with the same rules that day.
How do I change the language?
Open Settings › Language and choose English or Türkçe.
