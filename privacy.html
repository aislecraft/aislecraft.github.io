Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026 · Applies to Aislecraft 1.0 for iPhone and iPad

Aislecraft is a single-player puzzle game made by Okan Geter, an independent developer. The game works offline and does not require an account. The developer does not run servers for Aislecraft and does not receive your personal information. This page explains what the app keeps on your device and which Apple and Google services it uses.

On your device

Your progress, settings, the run in progress, finished results and reward records are saved on your device. They are not sent to the developer. Deleting the app removes them, and Settings › Reset local progress removes them from inside the game.

iCloud sync (optional)

If you turn on iCloud sync, your completed progress is stored in your own private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit. A run in progress stays on the device. The developer cannot read your private iCloud data. You can delete it in Settings › Delete cloud progress or in your device's iCloud settings.

Purchases

Aislecraft Premium and the Neon Mart theme are sold through Apple. Apple processes the payment; the developer never sees your payment details. The app checks Apple-signed purchase records on your device to unlock what you bought.

Game Center (optional)

If you sign in to Game Center, eligible scores and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center service. Apple's privacy policy applies to Game Center.

Advertising

Players without Premium may see optional rewarded videos in Classic mode and, after several finished Classic runs, an occasional full-screen ad when leaving a completed run. There are no ads during play, in the Daily Store, in Practice or in the tutorial. Premium removes all ads.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Aislecraft asks Google for non-personalized ads and does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, so your device's advertising identifier is not available to the app or to Google. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may process information such as your IP address (which can indicate an approximate location), device and app details, and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your choices through Google's consent form, and you can change them later in Settings › Ad privacy options. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Analytics and crash reports

This version of Aislecraft does not include analytics or crash-reporting services.

Permissions

Aislecraft does not ask for access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location, and it never asks for passwords.

Children

Aislecraft is a general-audience game. It is not directed to children, and the app limits ads to content suitable for general audiences.

Your choices and rights

You can control iCloud sync, ad consent choices and local data in the app's Settings. Because the developer does not hold personal data about you, requests about Apple or Google accounts are handled by those companies. For any privacy question or request, including under Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) or the EU GDPR, write to the address below.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, this page and the date at the top will be updated before the new version is released.
